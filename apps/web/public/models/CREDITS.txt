3D models used in the Mission Freefall build chapter (illustrations, not BASS-II flight hardware):

camera-still.glb  "Camera 01" by Rajil Jose Macatangay, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. https://polyhaven.com/a/Camera_01
                  Strap removed; textures converted to WebP.
camera-video.glb  "Infrared Camera", NASA 3D Resources. https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
                  Draco decoded; materials re-assigned in code.
glove.glb         "Astronaut Glove", NASA 3D Resources. https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
                  Draco decoded; textures converted to WebP.

The flow duct, fan, straightener, sample holder, igniter, radiometer, nitrogen nozzle, exit plate and
glovebox are modelled in code (components/three/FlameScene.ts) following NASA's BASS-II description.

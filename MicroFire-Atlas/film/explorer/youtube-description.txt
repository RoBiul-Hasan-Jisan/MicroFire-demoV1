AI voice disclosure: the voiceover is AI-generated (Microsoft neural text-to-speech, voice "Ava"). Our team wrote the script. Every number and quote comes from NASA technical reports.

On Earth, a flame stretches up because hot air rises. In orbit nothing rises, and a flame lives or dies on the air moving past it. MicroFire Atlas turns NASA's real microgravity fire experiments into an adventure for explorers aged 8 to 13, with the full evidence underneath for mentors and judges.

What's inside
- 76 test records: 56 BASS-II tests and 20 Saffire spacecraft-fire runs, each traced to its NASA table and PDF page
- 54 NASA findings, each quote checked word for word against its source at build time
- The Evidence Ladder: every question sorted into direct, analogous, mechanistic and gap evidence, with a "Why is this shown?" path on every card
- Predict-first comparisons between real tests (for example B20 → B16 as the airflow is turned down)
- A Research Frontier for NASA's proposed 34% O2 at 56.5 kPa habitat air, and open data downloads

Characters, scenery and the opening explainer are illustrations. NASA footage, photographs and test records are evidence. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

DESCRIPTION
AI voice disclosure: the voiceover in this video is AI-generated (Microsoft neural text-to-speech). The script was written by our team. Every number and quote in it comes from NASA technical reports.

MicroFire Atlas puts NASA's microgravity fire experiments on one map, test by test. It ranks the tests closest to a mission scenario and compares outcomes. It also shows where the evidence stops.

Live site: https://microfire-atlas.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/A-K-M-Asifuzzaman/MicroFire-Atlas

What's inside:
- 56 individual tests transcribed from NASA's BASS-II Summary Report (NASA/TM-20210011385), each linked to its PDF page
- 19 NASA findings, each checked word for word against the source when the site is built
- Mission Lab: ranks tests against a cabin scenario, including NASA's recommended 34% O2 / 56.5 kPa exploration atmosphere
- Evidence Gap Map: where tests exist and where they don't
- Ask: AI answers built only from cited evidence, with every citation checked
